Brief Bio

Xiuwen Yi (易修文) is a Professor at the School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence, Southwest Jiaotong University. His research interests include Urban Computing, Data Mining, and Spatio-Temporal AI, with a focus on developing data-driven methods to understand urban dynamics and support intelligent decision-making. Previously, he was a data scientist at JD, a postdoctoral researcher at Tsinghua University, and a visiting scholar at Penn State University. He has published over 40 papers in leading international conferences and journals, including KDD and IEEE TKDE, with over 5,000 citations. He has led over 10 research projects and serves as a Subject Editor for IET Smart Cities and an Area Editor for the International Journal of Computational Intelligence Systems. He was selected for the Beijing Nova Program (2021) and the CAST Young Engineer Program (2025).
